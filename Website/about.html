Who We Are

Built on Brick, Grounded in Trust

The company was founded in 1998 as Sharma & Co., a civil contracting firm based in Vasant Kunj, South Delhi, starting with residential flat renovations built on word-of-mouth trust in local colonies.

Over the years that followed, we took on work for well-known institutional and government clients including the Royal Bhutanese Embassy, the Ashoka Hotel in Chanakyapuri, and commercial projects in Noida building a reputation for reliability across both private and public sectors.

In June 2026, we formally renamed the company to Brickwood Developers, consolidating decades of contracting experience under one identity. That same year, we applied for MSME (Udyam) registration and obtained a Digital Signature Certificate to participate directly in government e-tendering.
