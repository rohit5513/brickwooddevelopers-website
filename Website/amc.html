Coverage Details

What's Included & What's Not

We believe in total transparency. Here's exactly what your AMC covers and what falls outside the scope.

Covered Under AMC

  • Plumbing: Leaking taps, running toilets, blocked drains, pipe joint repairs, tap washer replacement
  • Electrical: Faulty switches/sockets, MCB tripping, fan and light fixture servicing, plug point repairs
  • Civil: Wall crack fillings (hairline), plaster patches, tile regrouting, minor floor repairs
  • Carpentry: Door/window hinge tightening, cabinet handle replacement, minor wood touch-ups
  • Waterproofing check: Inspection of bathroom, terrace, balcony for seepage signs (Gold/Platinum)
  • Painting touch-ups: Spot repair of peeling/chipped paint areas (Gold: 1 room/yr, Platinum: 3 rooms/yr)
  • Labour: 100% of labour cost for all covered work during scheduled visits
  • Materials: Up to plan limit per visit (Silver ₹2k, Gold ₹5k, Platinum ₹10k)
  • Emergency calls: Dispatch within response window no extra call-out charge

Not Covered Under AMC

  • Major renovation work — full room makeovers, structural changes, new construction
  • Appliance repair/replacement — geysers, ACs, refrigerators, modular kitchen appliances
  • Full pipe replacement — concealed plumbing line replacement (only joint/tap repairs covered)
  • Full rewiring — complete electrical rewiring of flat or building
  • Waterproofing treatment — actual waterproofing execution (inspection covered; treatment quoted separately)
  • Full floor tile replacement — single tile repairs covered; full floor replacement is extra
  • Pest control — termite or pest treatment is not within AMC scope
  • Material cost overrun — materials exceeding per visit limit billed at actuals with client approval
  • Damage from misuse or natural disaster — flood, earthquake, fire damage falls outside AMC scope
