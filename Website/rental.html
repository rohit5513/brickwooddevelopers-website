What We Handle

Everything, End to End

From the moment your property is listed to the day the lease ends and everything in between.

Tenant Sourcing

We list your property on all major portals, conduct site visits with prospective tenants, and handle all enquiry calls on your behalf.

Tenant Screening

Background verification, Aadhaar check, employment/income verification, reference calls, and police verification coordination.

Legal Agreement

Drafting of rent agreement (11-month or long-term), notarised documentation, stamp duty guidance, and registration assistance.

Rent Collection

We collect rent by the 5th of every month and transfer it to your account by the 7th. Late rent? We follow up so you don't have to.

Maintenance Coordination

Any repair request from the tenant is routed through us. We assess, approve, coordinate, and invoice with your authorisation always taken first.

Monthly Reports

Every month you receive a clear statement rent received, maintenance spend, any deductions, and your net transfer. Full transparency, always.

Scope of Service

What's Included & What's Not

Included in Service

  • Property listing on NoBroker, MagicBricks, 99acres, Housing.com
  • All tenant enquiry handling calls, site visits, shortlisting
  • Background & Aadhaar verification of selected tenant
  • Rent agreement drafting (standard 11-month format)
  • Security deposit collection and documentation
  • Monthly rent collection and transfer to owner by 7th
  • Late rent follow-up with tenant on owner's behalf
  • Maintenance request routing assessment, quotes, owner approval
  • Move-in / move-out inspection with documented condition report
  • Monthly owner statement sent by 10th of every month
  • Lease renewal coordination and updated agreement drafting

Not Included

  • Guaranteed rent we collect and transfer; non-payment risk stays with owner
  • Court/legal proceedings against defaulting tenant (we assist but don't represent)
  • Stamp duty & registration fees for rent agreement (borne by owner/tenant)
  • Renovation / upgrade cost before listing charged separately
  • Society maintenance charges, property tax owner's direct obligation
  • Long-term lease registration (12+ months) additional fee applies
  • Insurance property or contents insurance not arranged by us
