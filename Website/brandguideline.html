Who We Are

Our Brand Philosophy

Mission

To deliver Delhi NCR's finest construction and renovation work — on time, on budget, with the same level of care for a 300 sq ft kitchen as a 15,000 sq ft government complex.

Vision

To be the most trusted name in Delhi NCR construction — the company every homeowner, business, and government body turns to when quality and accountability are non-negotiable.

Values

Craftsmanship. Transparency. Accountability. Punctuality. We do not cut corners, we do not hide costs, and we do not leave a site until the client is satisfied.

Brand Positioning

Brickwood Developers sits at the intersection of premium quality and accessible trust. We are not the cheapest option — nor are we the most expensive. We are the most reliable. Our brand identity must communicate this: understated confidence, earned credibility, and deep expertise.

Photography Standards

Photography is our most powerful brand tool. A poorly photographed project undermines 23 years of craftsmanship. Every project image must meet these standards before being used on the website, social media, or any marketing material.

Project Photography — Shot Types Required
🌅
Wide Establishing Shot
Full room, natural light
🔍
Detail / Craft Shot
Tile work, hardware, finish
↔️
Before / After Pair
Same angle, same lens
Photography Guidelines
✓ Always
  • →Shoot in natural light or with professional lighting
  • →Clean the space before photographing — no tools on floor
  • →Minimum 1920×1280px resolution
  • →Shoot before/after from the exact same position and focal length
  • →Include at least 1 detail shot per project (tile, hardware, finish)
  • →Save as JPEG 80% quality or WebP for web
✗ Never
  • →Low-light blurry photos taken on budget phone cameras
  • →Workers visible in project showcase photos
  • →Stock photos passed off as real projects
  • →Heavy Instagram filters that misrepresent colours
  • →Horizontal and vertical mixed in the same gallery
  • →AI-generated or rendered project images as real photos
Image Overlay System

When text is overlaid on project photography, always apply a gradient scrim: linear-gradient(to top, rgba(0,0,0,0.85), transparent) — bottom to top. This maintains text legibility while preserving image quality.

Voice & Tone

Brickwood Developers speaks with the quiet confidence of a company that doesn't need to shout. We've built for DDA, HUL, and Embassy Group. We let our work — and our words — do the selling.

Brand Personality

Expert, Not Arrogant

We speak from 23 years of experience. We don't need to boast — we state facts. "500+ projects delivered" not "Delhi's #1 contractor".

Not: boastful, self-important

Warm, Not Casual

We're professional but human. We talk like a trusted contractor who's worked with the family for years — not a corporate brochure.

Not: stiff, formal, distant

Transparent, Not Salesy

We tell people the price, the timeline, and what's not included. Our T&C is written in plain language. No fine print surprises.

Not: pushy, ambiguous, evasive

Reassuring, Not Timid

Clients are stressed about renovation. We calm them with clarity and process — "We respond within 4 hours" — not vague promises.

Not: overpromising, vague

Delhi, Not Global

We are a Delhi NCR company, through and through. We know Vasant Kunj from Rohini, Noida from Gurgaon. Our copy reflects this local authority.

Not: generic, global, placeless

Legacy, Not Nostalgia

Formerly Sharma & Co. We acknowledge our history proudly — it's 23 years of proof. But we are building forward, not dwelling in the past.

Not: stuck in the past, "old-school"
Voice in Action — Do & Don't
✓ This is how we write

"Get a free site visit and detailed estimate within 24 hours. We respond within 4 hours — no long waits, no chasing."

✗ This is NOT how we write

"Delhi's #1 premium luxury construction company!! Contact us NOW for AMAZING deals on renovation!! 🔥🔥"

✓ Service Description

"Flat Renovation. Complete transformation of your living space — flooring, walls, plumbing, electrical rewiring, and premium finishing. Delivered turnkey. Starting ₹350/sq ft."

✗ Service Description

"We offer world-class renovation services at unbeatable prices. Our expert team will transform your home into your dream space using top-quality materials!"

Response Time Language

Always specify exact times. Never use vague terms like "soon", "shortly", or "at our earliest". Our standard commitments: "We respond within 4 hours" (enquiries) · "We respond within 24 hours" (formal complaints) · "48-hour emergency response" (Silver AMC) · "4-hour emergency response" (Platinum AMC).

Digital Standards

Our website is our most important marketing asset. These technical standards ensure it loads fast, looks right on every device, and is found on Google.

Performance Targets

All pages must load within 2 seconds on a 4G connection. Measure with Google PageSpeed Insights.

  • PageSpeed Score: ≥ 85 (mobile), ≥ 90 (desktop)
  • Images: WebP format, compressed ≤ 150KB per image
  • Max 3 font files loaded (preconnect to Google Fonts)
  • No unused CSS/JS shipped to the browser
  • Lazy load all below-the-fold images

SEO Essentials

Every page must have correct meta tags for Google to index and display it properly.

  • Title: 50–60 chars · include "Delhi NCR" and service name
  • Description: 150–160 chars · include location + CTA
  • H1: one per page · must include primary keyword
  • Alt text: every image described (e.g. "flat renovation Vasant Kunj kitchen")
  • Schema: LocalBusiness schema on homepage

Accessibility (WCAG 2.1 AA)

The site must be usable by everyone, including those with disabilities.

  • All text meets 4.5:1 contrast ratio minimum
  • All interactive elements keyboard accessible
  • Form labels always visible (not just placeholder)
  • Focus states visible on all interactive elements
  • Skip-to-content link on every page

Mobile Responsiveness

70%+ of Delhi's internet users browse on mobile. Every component must be tested on small screens.

  • Minimum tap target size: 44×44px
  • No horizontal scrolling on any screen width
  • Font minimum: 14px on mobile
  • WhatsApp button always visible on mobile
  • Forms single-column on screens < 480px
