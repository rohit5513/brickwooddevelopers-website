About Brickwood Developers

Brickwood Developers is a construction, renovation, and interior solutions company dedicated to delivering high-quality residential and commercial projects. We specialise in construction, remodeling, renovation, interiors, and turnkey project execution with a focus on quality, innovation, and customer satisfaction.

Key Responsibilities

  • Supervise day-to-day construction and renovation activities at project sites
  • Ensure work is executed according to approved drawings, specifications, and quality standards
  • Coordinate with contractors, subcontractors, vendors, and labour teams
  • Monitor project progress and ensure timely completion
  • Conduct quality inspections and maintain safety standards
  • Prepare work schedules and project timelines
  • Coordinate with architects, interior designers, consultants, and clients
  • Verify contractor measurements and prepare BOQs
  • Monitor material procurement, consumption, and inventory
  • Attend client meetings and provide project updates

Required Qualifications

  • Bachelor's Degree or Diploma in Civil Engineering
  • Knowledge of construction methods, materials, and renovation processes
  • Ability to read architectural and structural drawings
  • Understanding of BOQ preparation and quantity estimation

Preferred Skills

AutoCAD MS Excel Quantity Surveying Site Management Vendor Management Quality Assurance Communication & Client Handling

What We Offer

  • Opportunity to work on diverse residential and commercial projects
  • Professional growth and learning opportunities
  • Hands-on experience in construction, renovation, and interior execution
  • Dynamic and collaborative work environment
  • Performance-based growth and incentives
