Renovation & Construction FAQs

What should I actually look for in the “best” construction contractor?

Transparent itemized pricing, a written warranty on structural work, a visible portfolio of completed projects, and genuine experience in your specific neighbourhood not just online ratings.

How much does a full home renovation cost in South Delhi?

It depends heavily on scope and finish level. We provide a detailed, itemized estimate after a free site visit rather than a generic per-square-foot number.

Do you handle society or RWA approvals for renovation work?

We coordinate the documentation side of a project alongside site work wherever the society or local authority requires it.

Can you manage a renovation while the home is still occupied?

Yes, we plan work in phases where needed to minimize disruption to daily life, particularly for kitchen and bathroom renovations.
