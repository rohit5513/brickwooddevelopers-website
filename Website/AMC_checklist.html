Arrival & Setup
Report to client / building society reception
Show ID card and AMC agreement copy if asked
Greet the client and confirm today's visit scope
Ask if anything specific needs attention first
Lay protective floor sheets in work areas
Protect tiles, carpet, and furniture before starting
Take "before" photographs of all rooms

📸 Photo
🚿
Plumbing Checkup
Check all taps — kitchen, bathrooms, balcony
Test for dripping, loose handles, low pressure
Replace leaking tap washers if needed
Use standard washers from kit — log material used
Material
Check toilet flush mechanism — all bathrooms
Test flush, check for running water inside cistern
Inspect under-sink pipe joints — kitchen & bathrooms
Look for moisture, white mineral stains = slow leak
Clear bathroom floor drain if blocked
Pour water to test flow — use wire hook if slow
Check geyser water inlet/outlet connections
Visual check only — do not open geyser body
Flag any major plumbing issue to coordinator
If scope exceeds plan limit — get client approval FIRST
⚠ Critical
⚡
Electrical Checkup
Test every switch in all rooms — on/off function
Use screwdriver to gently tighten any loose switch plates
Test all power sockets with a plug tester
Flag dead sockets — note room and position
Check MCB / fuse board — visual inspection
Look for tripped breakers, burnt smell, overloaded lines
Check all ceiling fans — wobble, speed, noise
Tighten canopy screws if fan wobbles
Replace fused bulbs / tube lights (if client has spares)
Do not purchase bulbs from your own kit — client provides
Check call bell / door bell — function test
Do NOT touch live wires, AC units, or concealed wiring
Report to coordinator — separate quote required for major work
⚠ Safety
🔧
Minor Civil Repairs
Inspect all walls for cracks — mark on sketch if found
Hairline cracks — fill with white cement. Wider than 2mm — photograph and report
Fill minor wall cracks with white cement putty
Max 3–4 small cracks under this plan. Log material used
Material
Check floor tiles for looseness — tap each tile
Hollow sound = loose tile. Mark and report. Do not lift without approval
Re-grout open tile joints if needed (2–3 tiles max)
Match grout colour to existing. Do not disturb full floor
Material
Tighten loose door hinges — all doors
Use proper screws — never leave a stripped hinge
Check window latches / handles — function test
✅
Wrap-Up & Departure
Remove all floor sheets and protective covers
Clean up all debris, dust, and material waste
Leave the site cleaner than you found it — no exceptions
Take "after" photos of all completed work
WhatsApp to coordinator before leaving site
📸 Photo
List all materials used and quantities to coordinator
This goes into the service report and billing
Report
Walk through completed work with client
Explain what was done and flag anything observed but not fixed
Get client signature on this checklist
